A Brief History of the World Wide Web

From its origins to the mobile era

01/ Creation

Tim Berners-Lee proposed the World Wide Web, introducing a system of linked documents accessible through browsers. This innovation transformed the way information was shared.

1989

02/ First graphical browser

The release of Mosaic, the first widely-used graphical web browser, made the internet accessible to non-technical users. Websites became visual and interactive.

1993

03/ social media rise

The rise of social media platforms redefined online interaction and content sharing. The internet evolved into a social space, connecting people worldwide and shaping digital culture and marketing.

2004

04/ smartphones & mobile web

The launch of smartphones revolutionized how people access the internet, putting powerful web browsing and apps in everyone’s pocket. Mobile-first design emerged, forcing interfaces to adapt to smaller screens.

2007
